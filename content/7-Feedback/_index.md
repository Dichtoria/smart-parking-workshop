Reflections, personal insights, and constructive feedback after an invaluable internship journey at the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program – AWS Vietnam:

### Overall Assessment

**1. Work Environment**  
The working environment at AWS Vietnam is highly professional, modern, welcoming, and open. The creative workspace at Bitexco Financial Tower inspired daily motivation. FCAJ community members and the AWS team were always ready to support, guide, and collaborate throughout the project lifecycle.

**2. Support from Mentors & FCAJ Admin Team**  
Highly skilled Mentors provided dedicated guidance, steering technical architecture toward best practices (AWS Well-Architected Framework). Mentors consistently encouraged independent problem-solving over providing canned answers. The Admin team promptly supported administrative procedures, lab account provisioning, and optimal hands-on conditions.

**3. Alignment between Work and Academic Major**  
The internship scope and the Smart Parking System Workshop project aligned perfectly with my Software Engineering / Information Technology major at FPT University. The project harmoniously combined web development, AI computer vision, and advanced AWS cloud services.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program delivered exceptional educational value: from mastering the AWS ecosystem (ECR, ECS Fargate, CloudFront, Amplify, RDS), designing Serverless/Container architectures, to essential soft skills like time management, technical pitching, teamwork, and professional communication.

**5. Corporate Culture & Team Spirit**  
The corporate culture is built on mutual respect, proactiveness, and equality. Team members maintained strong cohesion, shared technical knowledge, and assisted one another through debugging phases, fostering an incredible team spirit.

**6. Internship Support & Event Opportunities**  
Interns had direct access to technical seminars (GenAI, SageMaker, AI Agent, Bedrock), community meetups, and dedicated AWS Credit allowances for hands-on lab experiments.

---

### Experience Q&A

- **What were you most satisfied with during the internship?**  
  I was most satisfied with directly designing and deploying a production-grade system on AWS cloud infrastructure, learning directly from AWS Solutions Architects, and experiencing a global corporate work culture.

- **What areas do you think could be improved for future cohorts?**  
  The program could introduce additional advanced hands-on lab modules focusing on Cost Optimization and AWS WAF/Shield security during the initial weeks to accelerate student onboarding.

- **Would you recommend this internship program to peers? Why?**  
  I would 100% recommend the FCAJ program to fellow students. It is an ideal internship environment to gain real-world Cloud & AI experience, accelerating technical maturity and career readiness.

### Proposals & Future Aspirations

- **Experience Enhancement Proposal**: Organize additional team-building activities or internal mini-hackathons between FCAJ teams to foster technical networking.
- **Future Aspirations**: I aspire to continue engaging with AWS community events, technical workshops, and work toward becoming a professional Cloud/DevOps Engineer.
